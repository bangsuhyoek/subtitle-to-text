Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert an SRT file to clean text?

Simply drag and drop your .srt file into the upload box above, or paste its raw contents into the text box. The converter instantly strips sequential cue numbers, millisecond timestamps (like 00:01:23,456 --> 00:01:25,789), and formatting tags, assembling the dialogue into readable, well-spaced paragraphs.

How do I convert a WebVTT (.vtt) file to text?

WebVTT files contain header lines (WEBVTT), comment blocks (NOTE), styling rules (STYLE), cue settings (such as position:20% align:start), and speaker tags (<v Speaker>). Our parser automatically skips non-dialogue metadata, retains speaker labels if desired, and outputs pristine text.

How do I remove timestamps from YouTube transcripts?

On YouTube, click the three dots below any video and choose Show transcript. Copy the entire transcript or download the auto-generated caption file, then paste it here. Our tool strips all timestamps and automatically removes YouTube's overlapping duplicate lines.

Why do YouTube auto-captions duplicate words or lines?

YouTube generates rolling captions for live and auto-generated videos. Each subtitle cue repeats the previous cue's second line to create a smooth teleprompter scroll. Traditional text extractors create repetitive text like "hello world hello world how are you". Our intelligent deduplication detects and eliminates these rolling repetitions.

Are my subtitle files uploaded to a remote server?

No. All parsing and transcript formatting executes 100% locally in your web browser via client-side JavaScript. Your subtitle files, video scripts, and personal transcripts never leave your computer or touch any external server.
